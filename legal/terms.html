    2. The Apps and system requirements

    1. RF Analyzer is a software-defined radio (SDR) receiver. It displays and demodulates radio signals received with compatible SDR hardware (RTL-SDR, HackRF, Airspy, Airspy HF+, HydraSDR), from IQ files, or from remote receivers (rtl_tcp servers and KiwiSDR).
    2. RF Analyzer Digital Extension is an add-on for RF Analyzer. It decodes digital radio modes (for example ADS-B, FT8, POCSAG, AX.25 and LoRa) for RF Analyzer, which shows the results. It works only together with RF Analyzer installed from Google Play.
    3. The Apps are receive-only. They cannot transmit.
    4. System requirements: Android 9 or later. SDR hardware connected via USB requires a phone or tablet with USB host (OTG) support that can power the SDR. Remote receivers require a network connection. Whether a particular phone or tablet works with a particular SDR depends on both devices. Please use the free trial to check compatibility before purchasing.
    5. The features are described in the store listings and in the manual at https://demantz.github.io/RFAnalyzer/.
