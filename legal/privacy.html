    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: November 13, 2026

    This policy covers the apps RF Analyzer and RF Analyzer Digital Extension by Dennis Mantz (DM4NTZ). Contact: rfanalyzerapp -at- gmail.com (see also the Imprint).

    Neither app has user accounts, advertising, analytics or a crash-reporting service, and neither sends data to the developer. Google Play may give the developer anonymous crash and performance statistics, depending on your device settings. If you write to the developer, your message is used only to answer it.

    RF Analyzer

    Data on your device. Settings, bookmarks, station lists, recordings and the packets decoded in the Digital tab are stored on your device only. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes all of it. Recordings and exports leave the device only where you share or save them.

    Backups. If backups are turned on, Android includes RF Analyzer's settings and bookmarks in your device backup in your Google account. Your location, the KiwiSDR password, your recordings, the decoded packets and the app's log file are left out of it. When you move to a new phone directly from the old one (by cable or Wi-Fi), Android copies all of the app's data to the new phone.

    Location. RF Analyzer estimates your country on the device (mobile network, SIM, time zone, language) to choose band plans and station lists. Optionally you can choose your country, enter a grid locator or allow access to the device location (approximate, to about 2 km), for the maps, distances and nearby stations. Your location stays on your device: it is not sent anywhere and not included in backups.

    Internet connections. RF Analyzer connects only to the servers below. Each of them sees your IP address and what is requested, under its own privacy policy:

    • SDR servers you connect to: rtl_tcp servers and KiwiSDR receivers (a KiwiSDR's operator can see the frequency you listen to), and the public KiwiSDR list (kiwisdr.com)
    • map tiles from OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org) for the area you view
    • station lists: the digital station list (demantz.github.io, GitHub Pages; updated automatically) and, if you enable them, EiBi (afu-base.de), POTA (pota.app) and SOTA (sota.org.uk)
    • Google's time servers (time.android.com, time.google.com) for FT8's clock correction
    • Google Play, for purchases (Google Play version)

    The user manual is part of the app and loads nothing from the internet. Links you tap (for example QRZ.com, aprs.fi or FlightAware) open in your browser.

    Purchases (Google Play version). Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; the developer receives only the order information Google Play provides, no payment details.

    RF Analyzer Digital Extension

    The extension (com.mantz_it.rfanalyzer_digital) decodes digital radio modes for RF Analyzer on the same device. It has no internet access (it doesn't request Android's INTERNET permission). It processes the radio samples RF Analyzer passes to it in memory only and returns the decoded results to RF Analyzer, which stores and shows them as described above. The only thing the extension stores itself is whether you have seen its tutorial.

    Controller and your rights

    The controller under the GDPR is Dennis Mantz (address in the Imprint). The developer processes personal data only in two cases: when you write, your message and address are used to answer it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or (b) where it concerns a purchase) and deleted when no longer needed; for purchases, the order information Google Play provides is used to handle them (Art. 6(1)(b)) and kept as long as tax law requires (Art. 6(1)(c)).

    You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15-21 GDPR), and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority - for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg.

    Changes

    If the apps start to process data differently, this policy is updated first; the date above shows the last change.
