RF Analyzer turns your Android device into a real-time spectrum analyzer for
Software Defined Radio (SDR). Visualize and listen to radio signals around
you - from amateur radio to broadcast signals and beyond.

Whether you're a curious beginner or a seasoned ham, RF Analyzer offers a
hands-on, interactive way to explore radio signals.

FEATURES

- Built-in drivers for RTL-SDR (including the RTL-SDR Blog V4), HackRF, Airspy,
  Airspy HF+ and HydraSDR - no separate driver app needed
- No SDR at hand? Listen through public KiwiSDR web receivers all over the
  world, or connect to an rtl_tcp server
- View live spectrum (FFT) and waterfall plots
- Demodulate AM, FM, SSB and CW signals, with a squelch for every mode
- Scroll, zoom and tune through the bands - or long-press the spectrum for
  quick tune & zoom
- Record raw IQ samples or just the demodulated channel, and play IQ files back
- Band plans for your region, including the license-free bands
- Bookmarks for Stations and Bands
  - Import from SDR# or SDR++
  - Automatically synchronize with online lists (EiBi DB, POTA & SOTA Spots)
- Optionally starts when an SDR is plugged in
- A responsive and modern Material Design interface
- Built-in context-aware help and a full offline in-app manual

REQUIREMENTS

- Android 9 or newer
- For a USB SDR (RTL-SDR, HackRF, Airspy, Airspy HF+ or HydraSDR): a phone or
  tablet with USB OTG support and a USB OTG adapter (USB-A female to USB-C for
  modern phones; not needed for SDRs with a USB-C connector such as the
  HydraSDR)
- For KiwiSDR and rtl_tcp: a network connection (rtl_tcp streams several MB
  per second - use WiFi)

LEARN MORE
