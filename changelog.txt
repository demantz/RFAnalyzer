CHANGELOG - RF Analyzer

version 2.3
- Sources:
  - Built-in RTL-SDR driver (incl. RTL-SDR Blog V4): the separate driver app is no longer needed
  - KiwiSDR web receivers as a source, with a list and a map of the public receivers
  - Every USB SDR is recognized when plugged in; optional start when an SDR is plugged in;
    automatic restart after a short USB dropout
  - rtl_tcp and KiwiSDR ride out network stalls, their audio plays without gaps
  - Gain modes for Airspy and HydraSDR; fixes for HackRF, Airspy and HydraSDR
  - RTL-SDR without frequency limits (0 Hz - 3 GHz)
  - Android 17: local network permission for rtl_tcp and KiwiSDR in the local network
- Spectrum: true dBFS levels, automatic FFT size, smoother waterfall with much lower CPU use,
  quick tune & zoom (long-press empty space in the spectrum or waterfall)
- Demodulation: new mode picker, a squelch for every mode (in dB above the noise, keeps the start
  of a transmission), a tune step per mode, fixed CW, new audio path with less CPU use
- Recording: baseband recording (the demodulated channel); recordings continue in the background
  and when the app is swiped away; an interrupted recording can be kept at the next start
- Bookmarks: band plans for your ITU region, license-free (CB, PMR446, FRS, GMRS), ISM, maritime
  and air band lists, sharing, POTA/SOTA fixes, demodulation settings per station
- Privacy: location, KiwiSDR password and recordings stay out of Android's cloud backup; the
  manual has a Legal section and loads no fonts from Google
- Changed by the update: squelch settings start out off; the FFT size is chosen automatically
  (Manual FFT Size in the Display tab to pick one)
