    Digital Extension

    The Digital Extension adds decoding of digital signals to RF Analyzer: aircraft positions, pager messages, packet radio and APRS, mesh-network chats, FT8 contacts, teleprinter weather reports, tram priority telegrams and the station names and traffic messages of FM broadcast stations. Where Signal Demodulation turns a signal into audio, the Digital Extension turns it into text, numbers and positions that you can read, sort, search and put on a map.

    It is a free, separate app. RF Analyzer runs your SDR and shows everything that is decoded; the extension does the decoding in the background. It is available on Google Play only and works with RF Analyzer from Google Play; the open-source (FOSS) version of RF Analyzer has no digital modes.

    Digital modes are part of RF Analyzer's full version (also the Base Version together with the Full Version Upgrade). Without it - also with the Base Version - they have a trial of their own: two hours of decoding, counted only while a digital mode is received - looking at what was received is free, and it works even after the general trial has ended. The time left shows in a small pill above the control drawer, next to the general trial's operating time (tap it for details), at the top of the Digital tab's mode sheet and in the About tab. A message appears five minutes before the end. When the time is used up, the analyzer stops with a message; everything received stays viewable. Like the general trial, each start after that still decodes for 30 seconds, so you can still see a mode at work.

    Installing

    1. Install RF Analyzer Digital Extension from Google Play. Both apps must come from the same source (for example both from Google Play), otherwise Android doesn't let them talk to each other.
    2. Its launcher icon RFA Digital opens a short tutorial the first time. It shows how the extension works and checks that a suitable RF Analyzer is installed; its last page opens RF Analyzer on its Digital tab.

    After that, the RFA Digital icon takes you straight to the Digital tab. Long-press the icon and pick How it works to see the tutorial again.

    The extension has no screen of its own and needs no settings. RF Analyzer starts it whenever a digital mode is selected. Decoding keeps running in the background as long as RF Analyzer's notification is shown, just like audio demodulation.

    When Digital Modes Can't Be Received

    RF Analyzer never starts a digital mode that would silently receive nothing. When something is missing, the Digital tab, its mode sheet and the Demodulation tab show a notice that says what is wrong, with a button for the fix. Tapping Start or switching the mode list to Digital explains it too. Everything received before stays viewable.

    Notice What to do
    Digital modes trial used up Unlock digital modes shows the full version - or, if you have the Base Version, the upgrade - with its price; it unlocks digital modes for good. Until then each start decodes for 30 seconds.
    RFA Digital is not installed Install RFA Digital opens its Play Store page. Come back to RF Analyzer afterwards: it notices the extension by itself.
    Update RFA Digital The extension is too old for this RF Analyzer. Update RFA Digital opens its Play Store page.
    Update RF Analyzer RF Analyzer is too old for the installed extension. Update RF Analyzer opens its Play Store page.
    RFA Digital can't connect The two apps come from different sources (for example a self-built RF Analyzer). Install both from Google Play.
    Connection to RFA Digital lost RF Analyzer reconnects by itself within a few seconds. Nothing is decoded until then.
    RFA Digital is not responding Reconnecting failed several times within a minute. Tap Retry; if that doesn't help, stop and start the analyzer.

    While a digital mode runs, RF Analyzer checks every second that the extension is still decoding; a lost or stuck connection shows up within a few seconds. RF Analyzer's notification then says so too, so you notice it with the app in the background.

    Google Play sometimes updates one app hours or days before the other. Until both are updated, the notice stays, and FM with Stereo & RDS plays in mono.

    Starting a Mode

    There are two ways to start receiving a digital mode:

    • From the Digital tab (recommended): tap the mode button at the top left of the Digital tab, then › next to the mode to pick a band, city or network from the presets for your area (or Start to start the one you started last). RF Analyzer selects the mode, tunes the channel, sets the mode's settings, adjusts the source frequency and sample rate if needed and starts the analyzer. See Choosing a Mode.
    • By hand in the Demodulation tab: open the mode list (the Demodulation Mode button), switch to Digital and pick a mode. Then tune the channel to the signal like for any other mode. Each mode's page explains its settings.

    Only one mode is received at a time, analog or digital. Select your source first (an SDR, a KiwiSDR web receiver or a recording, see SDR Source).

    Decoded data appears in the Digital tab. Everything is stored on the phone, so you can come back to it later, search it, put it on the map and share it.

    The Modes

    The modes in the order of the mode list:

    Mode What you receive Typical frequencies
    RTTY Teleprinter text: the German weather service's broadcasts for shipping, radio amateurs shortwave, 147.3 kHz
    PSK31 Keyboard-to-keyboard chats of radio amateurs (also PSK63, PSK125) shortwave
    R09 The radio telegrams trams and buses send to get priority at traffic lights (Germany) 150-170 MHz
    FT8 The weak-signal mode behind most long-distance amateur contacts today shortwave, 6 m, 2 m
    AX.25 / packet radio APRS position reports, weather stations and messages; mailboxes and nodes of the packet networks 144.800 MHz (Europe), 144.390 MHz (North America), shortwave
    ADS-B Aircraft: callsign, altitude, speed and position 1090 MHz
    POCSAG Pager messages (paging networks, amateur DAPNET) 140-470 MHz
    LoRa The MeshCore and Meshtastic mesh messengers, LoRaWAN sensors, LoRa-APRS 868 MHz (Europe), 915 MHz (Americas), 433 MHz
    RDS Station names, radio text and traffic messages of FM broadcast stations 87.5-108 MHz

    RDS is received with the FM (wide) mode and its Stereo & RDS switch, so you can listen to the station at the same time.

    RF Analyzer only receives. It never transmits, and it shows only what is sent openly: encrypted messages (private mesh channels, LoRaWAN data) stay encrypted.

    Recordings

    The Digital Extension decodes recordings just like live signals: select the recording as the source and start the mode (see Recording & Playback). A recording made with RF Analyzer is a good way to try a mode again with other settings, or to look at a signal you caught while you were out. See Replaying Recordings for how the Digital tab treats the time of replayed data.

    When the Phone Can't Keep Up

    Decoding needs processing power, and some modes need a lot of it (wide LoRa channels, ADS-B). If the phone can't keep up, parts of the signal are dropped and packets are missed. The Digital tab then shows a Processing overload notice. See Processing Overload for what helps.
